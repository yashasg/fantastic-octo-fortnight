Frequently asked questions

Does kshana collect my data?

No. kshana stores settings locally on your device and does not use accounts, ads, tracking, third-party analytics SDKs, or a custom backend.

Why are reminders not appearing?

Check that reminders are enabled in kshana, notification permission is allowed in iOS Settings, and Snooze is not active.

What is True Interrupt Mode?

True Interrupt Mode is a planned Screen Time shielding feature. It will remain unavailable until Apple approves the required Family Controls entitlement.
